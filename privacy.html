Privacy Policy

Last updated October 5, 2026 · Applies to the Muzlo iOS app

Yevhen Basistyi ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains how Muzlo (the "App") handles information. By using the App, you agree to the practices described here.

01

Information we collect

Muzlo does not collect or store personal or sensitive data on our own servers. The App does, however, integrate with a small set of third-party services to provide song generation, crash reporting, advertising, and subscription management:

ElevenLabsGenerates songs, vocals and lyrics from the prompts you submit (elevenlabs.io).
Google AdMobServes in-app advertising.
Firebase CrashlyticsIdentifies and helps us fix app crashes.
RevenueCatManages in-app subscriptions and purchases.
02

Device permissions

Muzlo does not require access to your camera, microphone, contacts or location. Text prompts and lyrics you type are sent to ElevenLabs to generate your song; Muzlo itself does not retain a copy after that request completes.

03

How data is used

While Muzlo does not maintain its own user database, the third-party services above process data on our behalf:

  • ElevenLabs processes submitted prompts and lyrics to compose the song and return the audio and lyrics.
  • AdMob uses device and usage signals to serve personalized or generic ads.
  • Firebase Crashlytics collects crash and diagnostic data to improve app stability.
  • RevenueCat manages subscription state and purchase history tied to your App Store account.

No personal information is stored by Muzlo beyond what's required for these integrations to function, and nothing is shared without anonymization where technically possible.

04

Data sharing

We may share limited, anonymized data with:

  • ElevenLabs — to process song generation requests. No user-identifiable data is included in these requests.
  • Google AdMob — to display in-app advertisements.
  • Firebase Crashlytics — to diagnose and resolve crashes.
  • RevenueCat — to process and manage subscriptions.

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with any party outside of the integrations listed in this policy.

05

Data storage and security

Data generated by your use of the App remains on your device, with the exception of the prompts and lyrics sent to ElevenLabs for song generation at the moment you request it. Transmissions to our third-party partners are encrypted in transit.

06

Regional compliance

We aim to comply with applicable data protection regulations, including:

GDPR — European Union

Users in the EU may request access to, correction of, or deletion of data processed through our third-party services by contacting us below.

CCPA — California

California residents may opt out of third-party data processing by contacting us or adjusting the relevant settings within those third-party services.
