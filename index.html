About Muzlo

Everyone has a song in them. Muzlo helps it out.

Muzlo exists to close the gap between having a musical idea and hearing it — without instruments, software or years of practice.

The problem

Making music has always had a steep entry fee

Writing, arranging, recording and singing a song takes tools, training and time. Most people who want a birthday song, a video soundtrack or a tribute to someone they love never get to hear it.

The approach

Let words become music

You describe the song; Muzlo does the rest. Song generation is powered by the ElevenLabs API, which turns your prompt and lyrics into a finished track with vocals, while Muzlo handles the genres, artwork, library and player around it.

The team

Small, independent, and still shipping

Muzlo is built by a small independent team who make songs with it every day — every genre, screen and setting exists because someone on the team wanted it.

What we optimize for

Four things we won't compromise on.

Speed

Seconds, not sessions

From idea to playable song fast enough to still be excited about it.

Variety

Every sound covered

31 genres, from lo-fi to metal, so the song fits the moment.

Privacy

Your ideas are yours

No account details, no selling your prompts. Your library stays yours.

Simplicity

One clear flow

Describe, pick, generate. No timelines, no mixers, no manual.

Support

How can we help?

Answers to what we hear most. Can't find it here — email us directly.

How does Muzlo make songs?
You describe the song and choose a genre. Muzlo sends your prompt to the ElevenLabs API, which generates the music and vocals. Muzlo then adds a title, artwork and lyrics and saves the result to your Audio Room.
Can I write my own lyrics?
Yes. Write your own lyrics in the prompt, or describe a theme and let the AI write them. You can also switch on Instrumental to get a track with no vocals.
Which genres are supported?
31, including pop, hip-hop, rock, lo-fi, electronic, jazz, classical, country, K-pop and Afrobeats. See the Genres tab for the full list.
Can I choose how long a song is?
Yes — turn on Custom length on the New song screen to set the duration yourself.
Where do my songs go?
Every song is saved to your Audio Room, where you can replay it, read the lyrics, share it or delete it.
Is Muzlo free?
Pricing will be shown inside the app before you're asked to pay for anything. Subscriptions are handled through the App Store.
